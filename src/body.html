Loop500

A referral engine and live tracker built for one job: 500 final-year registrations in 7 days on ₹2,000.

Start here

Try the whole loop in about a minute

  1. Register as a student. The phone is the real flow. “Fill sample details” saves typing.
  2. Bring a teammate. Share your link on WhatsApp, or press “Simulate a teammate joining” and watch your pass unlock at 3.
  3. Open Mission control. Your registration is already counted, attributed to the captain whose link you came through, and in the funnel.

How this prototype works

What is realEvery number on this page is computed from an event log, not typed in. Registration, referral codes, tracked links, attribution, duplicate blocking, the three A/B tests and their statistics, the projection and the daily recommendation all run live in your browser.

What is simulatedThe 7-day campaign: fictional students at real campuses, generated from the plan’s own playbook with a fixed seed, so the week plays out the same way every time. Your own registrations are added on top and saved only in this browser.

What production needsA shared store so codes work across devices (a Google Sheet behind an Apps Script endpoint is enough for 500 rows), an n8n flow for confirmations and reminders, and one WhatsApp announcement group for broadcasts.
